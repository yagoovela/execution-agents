The rule that sets the order
The system is protected today by three accidents: the engine's sequential loop, one queued run per replica, and a blocking wait on every Temporal node. None was designed as a safeguard, and all three disappear when the v2 wave loop is switched on. Every wave that removes a throttle comes after the wave that adds the matching ceiling. That, and not preference, is what produces the sequence below.
