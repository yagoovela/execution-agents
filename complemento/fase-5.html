How it is today
The context is AsyncLocalStorage and goes blind the moment the handler runs in another process. The rest — status column, WebSocket signal, poll — is portable, but is not what you would build on Temporal.
How it will be
The backend calls handle.cancel(); the worker respects Context.current().cancelled. The poller and the ALS context are retired. Propagation to child workflows is native, which is exactly what phase 3½ will need.
The v2 got this one entirely right
The complement in this phase is only to quantify the gain and say what to retire alongside.
The cost of the current design
Sixty nodes hold sixty timers hitting the database. Behind a flag that is tolerable; on by default it becomes a performance offender.
